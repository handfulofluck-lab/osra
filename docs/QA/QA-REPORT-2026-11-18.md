**Two claims of mine that were wrong, both corrected by someone else's evidence.**
1. *The app id would be read by a parent.* I quoted `app_picker.dart`'s `label: id` fallback and asserted the
   reachable consequence. K31 showed the only caller that writes `appName` from a picker passes `disabled:`, not
   `selected:`, so the fallback cannot fire there. It was a contract defect, not a screen defect; I retracted it in
   the same comment that signed his fix (`6985088020`).
2. *`maxLines: 2` would hold a two-line sentence.* My proposal, not his. Flutter's `_RenderChip` caps a chip's label
   at one line (`maxHeight: contentSize`), so the cap only moved the cut to the screen the frames had not reached;
   removing the cap and the ellipsis was the correct instrument, and Codex's measurement (English needs three lines
   at 320/200 %) is what settled it.
