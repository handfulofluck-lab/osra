## Quick reminders before you start

1. **Use fresh wording for every test message (`PT-15`).** Sending the exact same message text from the same contact twice within 10 minutes is ignored as a repeat. Change a few words each time.
2. **Watch for the 1-minute refresh warning (`PT-10`).** The staging AI safety window renews once an hour. The team will warn you 1 minute before each renewal so you can pause sending test messages during the brief handover.
3. **What runs on which child phone (expected limits today, not bugs):**
   - **Chat scanning and OSRA's own AI model** run on **Android child 2 only** — iPhone does not allow any app to read messages from other apps.
   - **Web content filtering** runs on **Android child 2 only** — this iPhone child build uses Screen Time limits rather than a web content filter.
   - **iPhone location** uses **significant-change updates plus one-shot Locate now** (moves of around 100 m, roughly a 10-second wait after tapping **Locate now**, and fixes cached for up to 5 minutes), rather than continuous live tracking.
   - **iPhone apps & Insights:** on Flip4 parent, the iPhone child will show **no individual app list, no usage minutes, and no app insight sheet**. That is expected in today's build (tracked for follow-up in `#1046`), not a bug — iPhone app blocking works through **App groups on this iPhone** created on the iPhone with Apple's picker.
4. **Hands off until you finish (`PT-17`).** From Claude's 16:45 UAE go/no-go until you tell the team you are done, nobody on the team will touch any of the three phones. Behind-the-scenes merge, build, Firebase, staging, database, and AWS checks (`PT-01`, `PT-02`, `PT-03`, `PT-07`, `PT-08`, `PT-18` — not applicable for an Android parent — and `PT-19`) are already signed off before 16:45 UAE.
