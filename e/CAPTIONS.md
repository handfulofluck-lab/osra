# OSRA UX Contest — Option E ("One Calm Feed + Ask Bar") Captions

**Model E thesis:** *Everything that needs a parent arrives as an actionable card in one "Today" feed. Each card carries its own buttons, so most jobs finish without leaving home. Anything else is one tap into a focused page, or one sentence in the always-present **Ask bar** ("Pause Alex until dinner"). No tab bar.*

## Why Option E

1. **The feed is the to-do list.** `P-07-E` ranks cards by urgency: a flagged message (danger), a time request (amber), and live child status (teal). Each has inline actions (`Review`, `Approve +30m`, `Pause`), so the common jobs take one tap.
2. **Child filter chips, not separate homes.** `All · Alex · Sam` filters the same feed, so a multi-child family never switches screens to see who needs attention.
3. **Ask bar.** A persistent command bar lets parents type or say what they want. It sits alongside the screens rather than replacing them.
4. **Focused pages with one way back.** `P-08-E`..`P-23-E` are single-purpose pages that open from a card and return to `Today`, and the Ask bar stays on them.
5. **Child app mirrors it.** `C-01-E` is one calm "My day" page: a time ring, what's next, what OSRA checks (stated plainly), and one primary action.
6. **Privacy in the copy.** Excerpts stay encrypted until opened (`P-15-E`). "OSRA only flags concerning messages" (`P-17-E`). Retention is in plain words (`P-23-E`).

Palette: `#0B5D57` teal, `#F4F7F6` canvas, `#FFFFFF` cards, `#13211E` ink, `#4D5E5A` muted, `#FFF1D6`/`#8A4B00` amber, `#FDECEE`/`#A3242B` danger, 18px card radius. Rendered at `780×1688` (`390×844 @2x`).

> **Fictional data:** all names (Alex, Sam, Maya, Sara, Omar), devices, times, codes (`482 916`), sites and excerpts are synthetic demo data.

> **Arabic RTL:** `P-07-E-ar` and `C-01-E-ar` use `dir="rtl"` / `lang="ar"` with mirrored layout and Western numerals isolated (`1:42`, `3:00`, `1:18`, `16:00`).
