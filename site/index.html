Everything in one app

Protection that fits real family life

Set the rules once from your phone. OSRA applies them on every device your child uses, and tells you when something needs you.

Screen time

Daily and weekend limits, bedtime and school hours, and routines that allow or block apps at set times.

App limits

Block an app or give it its own daily limit. Your child can ask for access from the block screen.

Web filter

Block site categories and single sites, with SafeSearch and YouTube Restricted Mode. It runs on your child's phone.

Act right now

Pause the internet or the phone, lock it, make it ring, or give bonus time, and see when the phone confirms it.

Location and places

See your child's phone on a map, ask for a fresh location, and get an alert when they arrive at or leave a place you saved.

Chat safety alerts

In the chat apps you choose, OSRA checks the text on screen for signs of self-harm, bullying or grooming, and alerts you.

Checks use English phrases today. Arabic detection is in development.

SOS button

Your child can send you an SOS alert from a quick-settings tile, in one tap.

Tamper alerts

You are told if protection is switched off, and removing OSRA can be locked behind your parent PIN.

Ready in minutes

How it works

Setting up one device takes about five minutes. Keep your child's phone next to you.

  1. Install two apps

    OSRA on your phone. OSRA Kids on each phone or tablet your child uses.

  2. Pair with a code

    Scan the QR code from your app with your child's phone, then tap Allow when your app asks.

  3. Set the rules once

    Limits, bedtime, apps and web filter apply to every device of that child. Change them any time.

Questions parents ask

When can I get OSRA?

A closed beta starts in December 2026 on Android, iPhone and Windows. Email us to join it. Public release follows, and Mac comes in 2027.

How much does it cost?

Plans are announced at launch. You buy inside the app, through Google Play or the App Store, and one family plan is planned to cover your family on Android, iPhone, Windows and, when it arrives, Mac.

Will my child know OSRA is installed?

Yes. OSRA shows its icon and a notification on your child's phone at all times. It is made for parents and legal guardians supervising their own children, and must not be installed on anyone else's phone.

Does OSRA read my child's messages?

Only on-screen text in the chat apps you choose is checked for warning signs. Phone numbers and email addresses are masked on the phone before sending, and only flagged excerpts are kept, encrypted. The Google Play version does not read SMS, call logs or notifications.

Can two parents use it?

Yes. The family owner invites a second parent or guardian, who manages the same children with their own login.

How do I delete my account?

From the parent app, or by email. Everything is described on our delete account page.
