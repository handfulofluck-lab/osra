OSRA Kids Full: what it collects beyond the store app

DRAFT for approval. Not published until approved.

The OSRA privacy policy covers every OSRA app. This notice adds what the OSRA Kids Full app (com.osratech.child.full, downloaded from osratech.com) collects on top of the store app. Where the policy says the child app does not read SMS or call logs, that is true of the store app. The Full app reads the items below, and only after the parent turns them on and the phone grants the permission.

What the Full app adds

WhatWhat is sentWhat is never sent
CallsPhone number, incoming or outgoing, time and length, from the day the permission is givenThe audio. Calls from before that day.
SMSPhone number, time, and the first 120 characters of the message, from the day the permission is givenMessages from before that day. MMS pictures.
Message notifications from chat apps (WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat and others)The sender name and message text shown in the notification. They are checked the same way as on-screen chats: text that raises no concern is discarded at once, and text that does is kept as an encrypted excerpt of at most 240 charactersNotifications from other apps. Pictures, voice notes, files.
WhatsApp callsThe caller's name as WhatsApp shows it, time and lengthThe audio. The call's content.
Searches on YouTube and GoogleThe words searched for and the timeThe pages or videos opened after the search.

How long it is kept

The same as the main policy. Activity events (calls, SMS, searches) and stored message excerpts are kept for 90 days. Alerts are kept for 12 months, or until the child or the account is deleted if that comes first.

Updates

The Full app checks osratech.com/android/latest.json about twice a day. That request carries no personal data. The app installs only an update that is signed by OSRA and newer than the installed version.

Turning it off

A parent can turn each item off in the parent app. Removing the device in the parent app stops all collection, and deleting the account deletes everything, as described in Delete your account.
