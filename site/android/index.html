OSRA Kids Full for Android

OSRA Kids Full is the OSRA child app with three extra kinds of monitoring that Google Play does not allow. That is why it comes from this page and not from the Play Store. Install it on your child's Android phone, not your own.

Why it is not on Google Play

Google Play does not allow apps in its store to read text messages (SMS) or the call log unless they are the phone's main messaging or calling app. It also restricts reading other apps' notifications. These rules apply to every app on Google Play, including parental-control apps, so no store app can offer these features.

That is why leading parental-control apps offer two versions: a store version, and a fuller version downloaded from their own website. OSRA does the same. The Play Store version stays fully within Google's rules. The Full version is for parents who want these extra protections and choose to install it themselves.

Install it in five steps

A 45-second video of the steps below. It has no sound.

  1. On your child's phone, open this page in Chrome and tap Download OSRA Kids Full.
  2. When the download finishes, tap Open. If Android says Chrome is not allowed to install apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. Google Play Protect may warn that the app can monitor the phone. That is true, and it is what the app is for. Tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open OSRA. In your parent app, open your child and tap Pair a device. Scan the code with your child's phone, then tap Allow in your parent app when it asks.
  5. Follow the setup on your child's phone. It asks for each permission one at a time and says why it is needed.

After that, OSRA keeps itself up to date. If Android asks for permission to update, your child will see one notification: OSRA needs to update — tap to install.

Is it safe?

  • Only files from osratech.com are genuine. Do not install OSRA from anywhere else.
  • Every version is signed with the same OSRA key. Android refuses an update signed with any other key, and the app refuses to install anything it did not get from this site.

Be open with your child

OSRA is visible on your child's phone and shows when it is active. We recommend telling your child what it does and why. OSRA must not be used to monitor another adult or anyone you are not responsible for.
