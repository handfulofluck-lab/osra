OSRA Kids for Mac

The rules you set in the OSRA app, on your child's Mac, working alongside Apple's own parental controls.

The download button is added here with the first release.

What it does

  • Filters the web in every browser, with SafeSearch
  • Blocks the apps you block, and applies screen time and bedtime with a lock screen
  • Lets your child ask for more time
  • Checks which of Apple's protections are on, and helps you turn on the rest
  • Tells you if OSRA is stopped or removed
  • In Arabic and English

What it needs

  • A Mac with a recent version of macOS
  • An administrator account for you, and a Standard account for your child

Install it in three steps

  1. Sign in to the Mac with your administrator account and download OSRA Kids from this page.

  2. Open the installer. macOS asks you to allow OSRA's background service and web filter; allow both.

  3. When OSRA Kids opens, keep it on the pairing screen for the next step.

Pair it with your child

  1. On your phone, open your child in the OSRA app and tap Add a computer, then Mac.

  2. Type the six-digit code from your phone into OSRA Kids on the Mac, then choose a parent PIN.

  3. Tap Allow on your phone when it asks. The Mac now follows your child's rules.

Keep your child a Standard user

If your child's Mac account is an administrator, they could remove OSRA, and the OSRA app warns you. Give your child a Standard account.

OSRA Kids for computers sells nothing. Your family plan is managed in the OSRA app on your phone.
