Delete your OSRA account and data

Only the OSRA parent app has accounts. The OSRA child app has no account of its own: a child's data belongs to the family account and is deleted with it.

In the OSRA app

  1. Open the OSRA app and sign in.
  2. On the dashboard, tap the menu (⋮, top right), then Delete account.
  3. Type your email address, then your password, and your authenticator code if two-step sign-in is on.
  4. Confirm. The deletion happens at once and cannot be undone.

Without the app

Email support@osratech.com from the email address you sign in with, and ask us to delete your account. We reply to that address to confirm the request comes from you, then delete the account within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • If you created the family (the owner): the whole family. That is every child's location history, app use, alerts, message excerpts, rules and places, every linked phone, and every other parent's and guardian's access.
  • If you joined someone else's family: only your own account. The family and the children stay.

What is kept, and for how long

  • The identifiers of signed-out sessions, until those sessions would have expired (at most 8 days), so they cannot be reused.
  • Encrypted database backups, until they rotate out.
  • Server logs, which hold identifiers but no messages or content, until their retention ends.

Everything else is deleted at once. See the privacy policy for more.
