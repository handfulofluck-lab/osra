OSRA Kids for Windows

The rules you set in the OSRA app, on your child's PC: the same limits, bedtime and web filter as on their phone.

The download button is added here with the first release.

What it does

  • Filters the web in every browser, with SafeSearch and YouTube Restricted Mode
  • Blocks the apps you block, and applies screen time and bedtime with a lock screen
  • Lets your child ask for more time, and send an SOS
  • Tells you if OSRA is stopped or removed
  • In Arabic and English

What it needs

  • Windows 10 (22H2) or Windows 11, on Intel, AMD or ARM
  • An administrator account for you, and a Standard account for your child

Install it in three steps

  1. Sign in to the PC with your administrator account and download OSRA Kids from this page.

  2. Open the installer and follow it. Windows may ask for your administrator password.

  3. When OSRA Kids opens, keep it on the pairing screen for the next step.

Pair it with your child

  1. On your phone, open your child in the OSRA app and tap Add a computer, then Windows.

  2. Type the six-digit code from your phone into OSRA Kids on the PC, then choose a parent PIN.

  3. Tap Allow on your phone when it asks. The PC now follows your child's rules.

Keep your child a Standard user

If your child's Windows account is an administrator, they could remove OSRA, and the OSRA app warns you. Give your child a Standard account.

OSRA Kids for computers sells nothing. Your family plan is managed in the OSRA app on your phone.
