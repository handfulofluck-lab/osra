# C-05 first-run emulator evidence for PR #1208

Head `b8e3dd06b04410878b0aae618d9f5d9337a349c2`. Captured 27 November 2026 in UAE time.

The agreement uses a fictional 95-minute daily limit, bedtime, a school window, and three blocked categories. Pairing, permissions and Finish use display-only callbacks. The fixture requires no server or family data.
