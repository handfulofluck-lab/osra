### Child Android — collapse the notification shade while locked or paused (#828)
- While a parent `lock`, `pause`, or `pause_internet` is active (`LockState.isActive`), `AccessibilityMonitorService` collapses `com.android.systemui` shade expansions with `GLOBAL_ACTION_DISMISS_NOTIFICATION_SHADE` (API 31+) and three fixed-deadline follow-up pulses (`250 ms`, `700 ms`, and `300 ms` on content changes), keeping the volume dialog, power menu, biometric prompt, and keyguard bouncer excluded while focused and clearing stale `systemui` dialog classes when focus moves back to `LockActivity`.
- Incoming cellular calls remain answerable whether audible (`MODE_RINGTONE`) or silent/DND (`IncomingRingWindow` bounded 45-second upper-bound window from the default dialer or platform `incallui`, including One UI `com.samsung.android.incallui`), and removing the call notification (`NotificationCaptureService.onNotificationRemoved`) or posting a `CATEGORY_MISSED_CALL` notice closes that window immediately and triggers shade collapse without waiting out the 45-second bound, while non-essential calls and media are constrained under active device lock (`handfulofluck-lab/osra-security#33`, `handfulofluck-lab/osra-security#43`).
- `guardProtectionScreens` skips non-host packages before reading `rootInActiveWindow`, avoiding binder stalls during `systemui` shade teardown.
