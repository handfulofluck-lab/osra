### Child Android — collapse the notification shade while locked or paused (#828)
- While a parent `lock`, `pause`, or `pause_internet` is active (`LockState.isActive`), `AccessibilityMonitorService` collapses `com.android.systemui` shade expansions with `GLOBAL_ACTION_DISMISS_NOTIFICATION_SHADE` (API 31+) and three fixed-deadline follow-up pulses (`250 ms`, `700 ms`, and `300 ms` on content changes), keeping the volume dialog, power menu, biometric prompt, and keyguard bouncer excluded while focused and clearing stale `systemui` dialog classes when focus moves back to `LockActivity`.
- Incoming cellular calls remain answerable whether audible (`MODE_RINGTONE`, failing closed unless owned by a trusted platform dialer / `incallui`) or silent/DND (`IncomingRingWindow` single fixed 45-second monotonic `SystemClock.elapsedRealtime()` ceiling per active call from the default dialer or platform `incallui`, including One UI `com.samsung.android.incallui`), with essential-package exemptions preserved across notification, ring, and connected-call transitions in any event ordering; owner-matched notification removal (`NotificationCaptureService.onNotificationRemoved`), `CATEGORY_MISSED_CALL`, connected-call transition, and monotonic 45-second window expiry (re-arming on early wakeups) all run the same close-and-collapse path while non-essential calls and media remain constrained under active device lock (`handfulofluck-lab/osra-security#33`, `handfulofluck-lab/osra-security#43`, `handfulofluck-lab/osra-security#48`, `handfulofluck-lab/osra-security#49`, `handfulofluck-lab/osra-security#50`).
- `guardProtectionScreens` skips non-host packages before reading `rootInActiveWindow`, avoiding binder stalls during `systemui` shade teardown.
