- **Privacy and data screen (P-23).** Parent app: a new "Privacy and data" screen (Settings menu) shows what is kept and for how long (flagged message parts, alerts, location history) and where family data is stored, followed by the privacy actions: who can see data, getting a copy of the data (by email, as the privacy policy section 12 says), consent per child, and account deletion. The periods and the storage place come from the new `GET /v1/family/privacy`, which reads the same settings the cleanup job applies, so the screen can't show a period the server doesn't keep to. Deletion has no cancel window, so none is shown. English and Arabic.
