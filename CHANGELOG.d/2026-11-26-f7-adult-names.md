- **Adults' names in the child config (F7, for child screens C-01 and C-03).** The ruleset a child device reads or receives now carries `adultNames`: the family adults' display names, owner first, then in join order, at most 4, each trimmed and cut to 40 characters. Only names are sent, never ids, emails or roles. An empty list (or a failed lookup) means the child app shows its "your parents" fallback; the app chooses the wording with ICU plural/select. The parent's own rules read is unchanged. A renamed or newly joined adult reaches the phone on its next rules read or rules change.
