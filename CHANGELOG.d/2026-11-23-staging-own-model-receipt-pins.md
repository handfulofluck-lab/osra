- Staging own-model windows now render the four receipt pins the in-process scorer checks before every call (`OSRA_STAGING_QA_{V9,V9B}_{MODEL,THRESHOLDS}_SHA256`), from new optional `staging_qa_model_archives` fields `v9_model_sha256`, `v9b_model_sha256` and `thresholds_sha256`. They are rendered on the armed analyzer only, and only when all three are set. Without them every own-model call failed as unavailable and fell back to rules. Terraform tests cover armed, disarmed and a partial set.
