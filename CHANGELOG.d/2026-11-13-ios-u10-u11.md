### OSRA Kids for iPhone — "Close app" on the shield, and the PIN screen says why (U10, U11, #696)
- **U10:** the shield's second button read "Exit to Home screen", which wrapped onto two lines on the smallest
  iPhone. It now reads **Close app** (Arabic «إغلاق التطبيق», the Bureau's line of 13 Nov, the words both phone
  systems use). It still closes the blocked app. Android keeps its own line.
- **U11:** "Set a parent PIN" can't be skipped, so its first line now says why: **Set a parent PIN so only a parent
  can change OSRA Kids or pair this phone again.** (Arabic from the Bureau, 13 Nov.) The first sync already runs by
  itself behind this screen (UI/UX Bureau, from the Mac test bed's timings).
- Rows: `ShieldTextTests` pins both button lines, and `A11yIDTests` pins the reason line in both languages, placed
  before the PIN fields, with its own identifier (`pin.setReason`).
