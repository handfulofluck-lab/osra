- #1108 lets a parent who forgot the OSRA PIN on a child's Android phone set a new one from the parent app ("Change device PIN" under the phone's controls). The parent confirms with their account password, plus the authenticator code when two-step sign-in is on. The API checks the PIN against the phone's own rules, hashes it in memory and sends only the hash to the phone in a one-off, 15-minute command. A newer change supersedes it, and the hash is cleared on ack, supersede or expiry. Changes are limited to 5 per phone per hour. Every parent sees a "Device PIN changed" alert naming who did it. The old PIN is never shown, by the app or anywhere else. Built to Ahmed's R3 ruling (option A, conditions 1–7). The English and Arabic copy are drafts until the UI/UX and Arabic Bureaus sign off.
