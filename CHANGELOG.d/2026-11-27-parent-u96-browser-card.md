### Parent app — the "not ready" browser card names the screen and shows its state (U96, #650)

When a child's phone has not reported the web filter as running, P-12's browser card no longer prints a bare "Not blocking yet" sentence in place of the screen's name. It keeps the title "Web filter", carries the warning-coloured globe, and puts the existing warn pill on its own line beneath the title. **No new strings** — the UI/UX Bureau's decision at 12:46 was to reuse what already exists.

Verified by the `U96` widget test, with its mutant stated beside it.
