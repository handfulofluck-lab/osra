### Child agent (Android) — Doze-resilient periodic location refresh (#1053)
- Add `LocationRefreshPolicy` and `LocationRefreshWorker` (`CoroutineWorker`), scheduled in `WorkScheduler.scheduleAll` every 2 hours with a 30-minute flex window (`NetworkType.CONNECTED`, `ExistingPeriodicWorkPolicy.KEEP`) so an idle or overnight-sleeping phone refreshes location within `<= 2.5-3 h` across Doze maintenance windows instead of going 9+ hours stale.
- Schedule `PendingPingWorker.schedule(context)` directly in `WorkScheduler.scheduleAll` so freshly paired devices have the 30-minute Room pending-ping flush backstop without waiting for `BOOT_COMPLETED`.
- Track `lastLocationUploadedAtMs` in `AgentSettings` (cleared on `clearPairing()`), updated on 2xx uploads in `LocationService.flushPings()`, `LocationFixFetcher`, `PendingPingWorker`, and `LocationRefreshWorker`, so active moving phones skip redundant periodic location wakes (`SKIP_RECENT` while `< 2 h`, self-healing on backward clock jumps).
- Keep periodic refresh battery-safe and Android 14+ compliant: `LocationRefreshWorker` never starts a foreground service, checks `ConsentState.isPaused()` and `LocationStartPolicy.shouldStart` (requiring `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION` on API 29+), requests a single 15 s `PRIORITY_BALANCED_POWER_ACCURACY` fix (never `PRIORITY_HIGH_ACCURACY`), accepts `lastLocation` only when `<= 30 min` old and strictly newer than `lastLocationUploadedAtMs`, and uploads under `LocationService.FLUSH_MUTEX` with Room fallback on network failure.
- Verification scope: verified by Android JVM unit suites (`testFullDebugUnitTest` and `testPlayDebugUnitTest`) plus two planted-mutant checks; overnight Doze cadence on a physical handset remains pending hardware verification.
