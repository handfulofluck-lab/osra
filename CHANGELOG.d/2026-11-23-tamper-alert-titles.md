- Parent tamper alerts now say what happened (Karim, 23 Nov). Before, every tamper read "Protection tampered with" on the phone and in the app, including the agent's own "Agent updated" notice after every reinstall, which also woke the parent's phone. Now an `info` tamper (the update notice) stays in the Alerts list and badge without a push, like an `info` restart (#889); warning and critical tamper still always push. Each tamper `metadata.type` the agents and the server send has its own title in English and Arabic (agent updated, phone stopped reporting, protection turned off, uninstall protection off, removal attempt, parent PIN locked, web filter disconnected or bypassed, block screen permission removed), and the local notification uses it too. Unknown types keep the generic title.
