### Parent app — a coaching card names a real number, or a sentence with none (#723)
- The coaching cards carried `[add local number]` / `[أضف الرقم المحلي]` in four sentences, in both languages, including the self-harm and grooming cards. Four tokens replace them: `{emergency}`, `{crisis}`, `{childProtection}`, filled at render time from `apps/parent-app/lib/features/coach/local_help_numbers.dart`.
- The numbers are looked up by the parent's device country (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 from `PlatformDispatcher.locale.countryCode`), because no country is stored on the family yet. A region (`US`/`EU`/`UK`) fills the **emergency number only** — 911, 112, 999 — because child-protection and crisis lines differ inside a region; `OTHER` fills nothing.
- A field with no number becomes a sentence with no digits in it: "call your local emergency number", «اتصل برقم الطوارئ المحلي», "contact your local child-protection service". A wrong number in a self-harm card is worse than no number, so the table stays empty until Claude's rows land with one official source URL per number.
- New test `test/local_help_numbers_test.dart` (14 rows): the country row wins over the region, a region never fills the child-protection or crisis field, an unknown country renders the sentence in both languages, and a scan of every `.dart` file under `lib/` fails the build if either placeholder string comes back.
