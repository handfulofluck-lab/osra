### Parent app — a coaching card names a real number, and the hours it answers (#723, #727)
- The coaching cards carried `[add local number]` / `[أضف الرقم المحلي]` in four sentences, in both languages, including the self-harm and grooming cards. Four tokens replace them: `{emergency}`, `{crisis}`, `{childProtection}`, filled at render time from `apps/parent-app/lib/features/coach/local_help_numbers.dart`.
- The numbers are looked up by the parent's device country (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 from `PlatformDispatcher.locale.countryCode`), because no country is stored on the family yet. A region (`US`/`EU`/`UK`) fills the **emergency number only** — 911, 112, 999 — because child-protection and crisis lines differ inside a region; `OTHER` fills nothing.
- **The country table now ships the eleven rows Claude sourced on #723:** AE, SA, QA, KW, BH, OM, JO, EG, TR, US, GB, each with one official source URL in the comment above it. An *empty* or *hold* cell stays `null`, and that field still renders the sentence ("call your country's child-protection service") rather than a guessed number.
- **Each help line states its opening hours**, in the Bureaus' words, inside the clause after the number. Arabic is the Arabic Language Bureau's (#727 comment 6869947527), English the UI/UX Bureau's (6869971353). Hours are held **per line**, not per row: Saudi Arabia's child line runs 07:00–23:00 and its crisis line 08:00–20:00 plus Saturdays, so one pair per row would print the wrong bracket after the other line.
- 24-hour lines carry `(open 24 hours)` / «(يعمل على مدار الساعة)»; a line whose source states no hours shows **no bracket at all**, and "not stated" is never read as 24 hours (Claude, #727 6869832567).
- Qatar's `16000` keeps its step before the bracket — `ثم اختر الرقم 4` / "and choose option 4" — so the dialled number stays a number.
- **UK ruling (#727 comment 6869989999):** NSPCC helpline 0808 800 5000 is **Monday to Friday 10:00–16:00**, from `https://learning.nspcc.org.uk/services/nspcc-helpline`. The older 08:00–22:00 with weekends in #723 could not be confirmed and is gone.
- A shown number uses U+00A0 in place of its ordinary spaces inside the LTR isolate (UI/UX Bureau), so `0808 800 5000` cannot break across two lines; the value that is dialled or copied keeps ordinary digits and spaces.
- The card still never works out whether a line is open: no "closed now", only the hours the Bureaus worded. A wrong "closed" would stop a parent calling a line that answers.
