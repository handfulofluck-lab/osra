### Parent app — social monitoring states what the child's phone reports (#1179, P-17)
- The per-app switch is gone from Social monitoring. Each app row now shows the phone's own word for the check —
  ready, limited, not available or not in this version — read from the child's capability report, and "Off"
  when the parent turned that app off. Tapping the row opens the two choices instead of flipping a switch that
  promised coverage the phone had not reported.
- A child on an iPhone sees "Not yet available on iPhone" instead of a list of apps nothing scans.
- No lock step was added: Karim's decision is that flagged parts open directly in the parent app, and the app
  PIN stays removed.
