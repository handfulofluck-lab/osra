- **New children start with no routines running (#1172).** A newly added child used to get School hours (08:00–15:00, Monday to Friday) and Bedtime (21:00–07:00) switched on before the parent chose anything, and School hours could block the phone in the middle of setup. Now a new child starts with Bedtime off and no school days. Bedtime has an on/off switch in the rules editor, and its times are kept, so switching it on restores them. Children added before this change keep what they have. When Bedtime is off, the server sends the phones blank bedtime times, which every child build (Android, iPhone, Windows) already treats as "no bedtime", and the iPhone child also stops a bedtime it scheduled earlier.
