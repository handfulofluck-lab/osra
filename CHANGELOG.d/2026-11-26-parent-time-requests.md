### Parent app — Answer a daily-limit request (#1179)

- The Requests section and full alert show the requested time. A caregiver can add 15 or 30 minutes for the day, or choose “Not today.” The result shows the first caregiver’s decision and when they made it. Request timestamps follow the language’s reading direction.
- The P-20 screen is behind `OSRA_UX_P20`. Flutter checks cover the actions, access requests, refresh, and text wrapping. A paired-device grant check is still required before the flag is enabled for testing.
