### Parent app — P-04 shared add-child flow (#1179)
- Under `OSRA_UX_P04`, the onboarding wizard and dashboard Add child action use one name-and-age form. The legacy dashboard dialog remains unchanged when the flag is off.
- Age is validated from 1 to 17 against a shared clock and sent as `birthYear = currentYear - age`; the family's own region is used for consent jurisdiction with no US fallback or jurisdiction picker.
- Guardian affirmation starts unticked and is required before the single child-creation POST. The privacy collection notice opens in a bottom sheet without losing the form. No draft, starter-rule template, server change, or extra POST is added.
- Includes final UI/UX and Arabic Bureau copy, per-field validation, and P-07's shared `openAddChildFlow` integration point for when that screen lands.
