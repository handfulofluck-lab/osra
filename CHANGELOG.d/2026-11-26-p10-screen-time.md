- **Screen time screen (P-10).** Parent app: a new "Screen time" screen (from the child's page) shows how much time is left today against the limit the child's phone applies today (the school-day limit, or the weekend limit on Saturday and Sunday, the same rule the Android child uses), adds today-only extra time (+15, +30, +60) or pauses the internet, changes the school-day and weekend limits in 15-minute steps, links to the apps that never count, and lists where today's time went by app name. Each stepper saves only the limit it changes. iPhone children get a "Not yet available on iPhone" line, because iPhone doesn't report minutes. The existing extra-time and wind-down cards on the child's page stay as they are. English and Arabic.
