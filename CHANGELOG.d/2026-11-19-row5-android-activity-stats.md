### API & Android — reports summary reads durable telemetry and DNS rewrites record site visits (#830, #841)
- `GET /v1/reports/summary` (`summarizeChildActivity`) and `GET /v1/reports/wellbeing/:childId` now fold durable `AppUsageDaily` totals and per-app breakdowns (mapped to launcher labels from `DeviceApp` when reported) alongside `ActivityEvent` without double-counting, closing the 9 min vs 2,797 min split between `ReportsScreen` and `UsageHistoryScreen` observed in the 19 Nov staging run. When `SITE_NAMES_ENABLED=true`, `summarizeChildActivity` also folds `WebVisit` rows while keeping site names completely hidden while the rollout flag is off.
- Android `UsageReporter` serializes uploads behind `uploadMutex`, initializes `KEY_WATERMARK` on empty first runs so `UsageStatsManager` baselines immediately, writes a 4-minute interactive foreground heartbeat and a window-boundary continuation anchor (`continuationPackage`) so continuous video/reading sessions are not truncated at 5 minutes or dropped across windows, and flushes immediately on `Intent.ACTION_SCREEN_OFF` without adding idle network traffic.
- Android `FilterVpnService` now records queried hosts on `DnsFilterPolicy.Action.REWRITE` (`google.*`, `bing.com`, `duckduckgo.com`, `youtube.com` under SafeSearch and YouTube Restricted Mode, gated by `SiteVisitGate` behind `siteNamesEnabled`), shortens batch flush latency to 15 s, and flushes queued visits on screen-off. Verified with unit and route suites on Windows; live handset verification awaits the 07:00 UAE staging cut.
