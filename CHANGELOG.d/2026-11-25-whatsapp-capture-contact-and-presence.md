### Child Android — WhatsApp burst dedupe, sender mapping and presence filtering (#1141, #1140)
- Preserves every decoded `MessagingStyle` line while preventing a repeated line from being queued again merely because its notification timestamp changed. Message senders now supply the contact before conversation and notification title fallbacks. Replay identity is sender + text, so two people saying the same line both arrive while a repeated snapshot adds nothing.
- WhatsApp presence/activity notices (`online`, typing and recording-audio forms across the existing WhatsApp UI locales) are no longer scanned as chat messages, preventing a status word from becoming a false concern contact. The non-message category rejection is scoped to WhatsApp; other monitored apps keep their previous notification behavior.
- Android unit coverage uses a `Bundle[]` burst, per-line senders, duplicate redelivery, localized presence notices and a following real message. Hardware verification remains a separate device-gate claim.
